## The problem

Polished screen-recording tools bake the cursor, zoom and background into the video at capture
time. If the zoom lands wrong, you record again. I wanted to record once and decide later.

## What it does

- **One shortcut (⌘⇧2)** to record a display, a window or an area, with system audio and mic as
  separate tracks. Pause cuts the paused time out of the movie.
- **An editor** for background (wallpaper, gradients, image), frame (aspect, padding, radius,
  shadow), cursor (style, size, smoothing, click ripples) and auto zoom segments you drag on a
  timeline.
- **Export** to MP4 (HEVC or H.264, 720p to 4K) or GIF. Preview and export share one renderer, so
  what you see is what you get.

## Architecture

- **Capture:** ScreenCaptureKit with the cursor excluded, frames written straight to HEVC with
  AVAssetWriter. Audio stored as Float32 so both tracks survive intact until export.
- **Event timeline:** global mouse monitors log cursor positions, clicks and scrolls; each cursor
  shape is stored once; the front window frame is sampled 10× per second. All timestamps share the
  video&rsquo;s host clock.
- **Rendering:** an AVFoundation video composition whose per-frame handler runs a Core Image
  compositor (background, shadow, camera crop, rounded frame, cursor, ripples), used by both
  AVPlayer preview and export.
- **Format:** a `.zoomcast` package holding `raw.mov`, `events.json`, cursor PNGs and an editable
  `project.json`.

## Interesting problems I solved

**Auto zoom that feels hand-made.** Clicks are clustered into camera keyframes: ease in 350 ms before
the first click, pan between clicks, hold 1.5 s, ease out. Segments stay editable.

**Pause without drift.** On resume, every later timestamp (video, audio and event timeline alike) is
shifted back by the paused duration, so cursor overlays never slip out of sync.

**Two distribution channels from one codebase:** a notarized Developer ID DMG and a sandboxed Mac
App Store target, with the release pipeline automated through fastlane and Cloudflare R2.
