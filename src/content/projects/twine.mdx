## What it does

One shared space for two people, on iOS and Android from a single codebase.

- **Pairing** with a 6-character invite code.
- **Dates:** shared calendar, propose and approve flow, surprise dates, native calendar sync, and
  create-a-date-from-a-picture using an LLM.
- **Wishes** with categories, locations and map view; a **journal** of photo memories; a shared
  **watchlist** of shows and movies.
- Push and in-app notifications.

## Privacy

End-to-end encrypted. Content is encrypted on the two devices with libsodium, so the server only
holds ciphertext and routing metadata. Recovery uses a 12-word phrase plus partner re-pair.

## Architecture

- Flutter client; Firebase Auth for identity only.
- Cloudflare Workers with D1 for data, R2 for encrypted media and Durable Objects for real-time state.
- Released through both stores, currently on version 1.4.
